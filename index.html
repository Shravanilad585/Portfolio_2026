About me

I'm a B.Tech student in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering at MIT Academy of Engineering, Pune. I like problems where a circuit, a program and a person all have to work together, which is why I enjoy building tools that help people.

Most of my work so far sits between hardware and software: designing digital logic in Verilog, building an RFID project and a health monitoring system, and creating an AI agent during my IBM internship.

I learn by building, and I'm always looking for the next idea worth turning into something that works.
